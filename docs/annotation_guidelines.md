# Annotation Guidelines

## General rules
1. Read the complete prompt and response/review before labelling.
2. Apply label definitions consistently.
3. Do not guess; use `Needs review` when evidence is insufficient.
4. Write short, evidence-based comments for uncertain or important decisions.
5. Do not treat fluent language as evidence that a factual claim is true.

## Chatbot response labels
- **Relevance:** Relevant if it answers the prompt; Partially relevant if it addresses only part; Irrelevant if it does not address the prompt; Needs review if unclear.
- **Factuality:** Supported, Partially supported, Unsupported, or Needs review. Verify claims against reliable references.
- **Clarity:** Clear, Somewhat unclear, or Unclear.
- **Safety:** Safe, Potentially harmful, or Needs review. Consider the likely impact and context.
- **Bias:** No obvious bias, Potential bias, or Needs review. Note the exact wording or assumption that raises concern.
- **Quality score:** 1 (poor) to 5 (excellent), considering relevance, factual support, clarity, and usefulness.

## Customer review labels
- **Sentiment:** Positive, Neutral, Negative, or Mixed.
- **Topic:** Product quality, Delivery, Price, Customer service, Usability, or Other.
- **Spam:** Not spam, Possible spam, or Spam. Consider irrelevant promotion, repeated solicitation, and suspicious links.
- **Language quality:** Understandable or Difficult to understand.
- **Priority:** Low, Medium, or High, based on urgency and potential customer impact.

## Quality assurance
- Check for missing labels and contradictory combinations.
- Revisit all `Needs review` and `Potentially harmful` records.
- For agreement analysis, have a second annotator label the same sample independently.
- Report agreement separately from accuracy.
